\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

Eigenfaces \citep{turk1991} is the reference appearance-based method; its linear descendants replaced PCA's unsupervised criterion with label-aware objectives, and its failure modes motivated decades of illumination normalisation. Our Map A is deliberately the plain 1991 recipe, because the point is to measure what the raw representation contains. The deep lineage from DeepFace \citep{taigman2014} through FaceNet \citep{schroff2015}, VGGFace2 \citep{cao2018}, and ArcFace \citep{deng2019} is surveyed by \citet{wang2021}; NIST's FRVT evaluates submitted systems on millions of images at fixed FAR \citep{grother2019}. On measurement, the visualisation-versus-embedding distinction is well known \citep{maaten2008}, but the observation that the \emph{worse} representation can have the more faithful 2-D plot is a useful cautionary result. Small, fully specified replications are valuable precisely because they are inspectable.
